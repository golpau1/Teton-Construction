85 Mountain View

Miami

Status
Current Construction
Completion
2027
Client
Elemence Group
Location
Miami

A boutique collection of four architecturally designed townhouses, moments from the beach in the elevated, evolving pocket of Miami on the Gold Coast. Each three-storey home is crowned by a private rooftop terrace with pergola and built-in barbecue — coastal living, refined.

Inside, open-plan living, a butler's pantry and a top-floor primary suite with balcony are finished in natural stone, engineered timber and a considered material palette, framed by a brick and render façade. Secure double parking to every residence — four homes, thoughtfully finished, in a location that speaks for itself.
